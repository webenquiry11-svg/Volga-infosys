FAQ

Common Questions About Our Industry Solutions

Have questions about how we tailor immersive tech to your sector? We've got answers.

How long does it take to build a custom industry solution?

Timelines vary based on scope, but most projects take 8-16 weeks from concept to launch. We'll give you a detailed timeline during your free consultation.

Do you integrate with our existing systems?

Absolutely! We design our solutions to seamlessly integrate with your current LMS, CRM, ERP, and other enterprise systems.

What hardware do your solutions require?

We build for all major VR/AR headsets, as well as web-based experiences that work on any device — no special hardware needed.

Can you help with training our team to use the solution?

Yes! We provide comprehensive training for your team, as well as ongoing support and maintenance packages.
